# HD-05 · Hand off a Microsoft 365 MFA sign-in issue

**GLPI #5** · Tabletop incident · Medium priority in the exercise · Pending with Tier 2

A fictional Microsoft 365 user had lost access to her MFA device. I asked whether she could complete the challenge another way. She reported that she had no alternate method.

I scoped the issue to one user and sent Tier 2 the affected service, time zone and a request to verify identity and handle MFA recovery. I told the requester who would take over and to be ready for identity verification. The ticket remained pending at handoff.

The case records a Tier 1 triage decision and a customer update. The exercise ended before a recovery action or successful cloud sign-in.
